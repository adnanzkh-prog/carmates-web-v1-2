Built on Mateship.

Founded in Melbourne, CarMates was born from a simple realization: the car industry had lost its soul. Buying a car shouldn't feel like a battle—it should feel like a handshake between friends.

Our mission is to restore trust, reliability, and peace of mind to the Australian car market. We treat every vehicle like it’s going to our own family, and every customer like our best mate.
